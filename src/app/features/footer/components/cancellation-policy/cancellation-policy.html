Cancellation & Refund Policy

This Cancellation & Refund Policy outlines the terms under which patients may cancel appointments, request refunds, or modify scheduled healthcare services offered by Aabind Hospital. Our goal is to provide a transparent, fair, and patient-friendly process while ensuring efficient management of healthcare resources.

1. Patient-Initiated Cancellation

Patients may cancel an appointment, consultation, diagnostic booking, or healthcare service before the scheduled start time through:

  • check_circle The Aabind Hospital website or patient portal.
  • check_circle Mobile application, if applicable.
  • check_circle Hospital reception or appointment desk.
  • check_circle Customer support channels.

Cancellation requests are subject to the timeframes and conditions outlined in this policy.

2. Cancellation Timeframes

Time of Cancellation Policy
24 hours or more before scheduled service Full cancellation allowed with no charges.
Less than 24 hours before scheduled service Cancellation fee may apply.
After service commencement or no-show No cancellation or refund permitted.

Aabind Hospital reserves the right to determine applicable cancellation fees based on the type of service booked.

3. How to Cancel a Booking

Online Cancellation

  1. 1 Log in to your patient account.
  2. 2 Navigate to My Appointments or My Bookings.
  3. 3 Select the appointment or service you want to cancel.
  4. 4 Click Cancel Booking and follow the instructions.

Offline Cancellation

Patients may contact Aabind Hospital directly for cancellation assistance through the hospital reception or customer support team.

4. Hospital-Initiated Cancellation

In rare circumstances, appointments or services may need to be cancelled due to:

  • medical_services Doctor unavailability.
  • emergency Medical emergencies.
  • build Equipment maintenance or technical issues.
  • cloud Natural disasters or unforeseen events.
  • business Operational or administrative constraints.

When such cancellations occur, patients will be informed promptly and offered:

  • check_circle A full refund, or
  • check_circle An alternative appointment date and time.

5. Modification & Rescheduling Policy

Patients may request changes to existing bookings subject to the following conditions:

  • check_circle Availability of doctors or healthcare professionals.
  • check_circle Availability of diagnostic equipment or service slots.
  • check_circle Compliance with the applicable cancellation timeframe.

Rescheduling requests made after the permitted period may not be accommodated.

Aabind Hospital reserves the right to decline rescheduling requests where operational limitations exist.

6. Refund Policy

Eligibility for Refunds

Full Refund

  • check_circle The appointment is cancelled within the eligible cancellation period.
  • check_circle Aabind Hospital cancels the service.
  • check_circle Payment was processed incorrectly due to a technical issue.

Partial Refund

  • info A cancellation occurs outside the standard cancellation window.
  • info Administrative or service-related charges have already been incurred.

No Refund

  • cancel Completed consultations or healthcare services.
  • cancel Missed appointments or no-shows.
  • cancel Services already rendered.
  • cancel Diagnostic tests already performed.
  • cancel Last-minute cancellations beyond the permitted timeframe.
  • cancel Promotional, discounted, or special package bookings unless otherwise stated.

Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5–10 business days. Actual credit timelines may vary depending on the banking institution, payment gateway provider, credit card issuer, or UPI service provider.

Refund Method

Refunds will be credited to the original payment method used during booking, including:

  • credit_card Credit Cards
  • credit_card Debit Cards
  • account_balance UPI Payments
  • account_balance Net Banking
  • account_balance_wallet Hospital Wallet or Credit Balance, where applicable
