01 Delivery Coverage

Delivery services are available in selected cities, towns, and serviceable pin codes.

  • Delivery availability is displayed during checkout based on the delivery address.
  • Coverage depends on the operational reach of Aabind Hospital and its pharmacy partners.
  • Certain specialized medicines, medical devices, and temperature-sensitive products may have limited delivery availability because of storage and handling requirements.

02 Delivery Timeline

Delivery Type Estimated Delivery Time Availability

⚠️ Important Notice

Delivery timelines are estimates and are not guaranteed. Delays may occur due to high order volume, severe weather conditions, traffic disruptions, public holidays, operational constraints, or regulatory requirements. Patients will be informed promptly of significant delays.

03 Order Processing

Order Confirmation

Orders are considered confirmed only after successful payment authorization and verification.

Prescription Verification

Orders containing prescription medicines regulated under applicable laws require a valid prescription issued by a registered medical practitioner. Orders that fail verification will be cancelled and refunded where applicable.

Medicine Substitution Policy

If a prescribed medicine is unavailable, patients will be notified. Any substitute medicine requires patient approval before dispatch.

Order Modification

Orders may be modified or cancelled before dispatch confirmation. Once dispatched, modifications are generally not possible.

06 Returns & Refunds

Non-Returnable Products

  • Prescription medicines
  • Controlled or regulated substances
  • Temperature-sensitive or cold-chain products
  • Opened, used, or partially consumed products
  • Products with broken seals or tampered packaging
  • Personal hygiene products
  • Single-use medical devices

Eligible Refund or Replacement Cases

  • Incorrect product delivered
  • Expired product delivered
  • Damaged product received
  • Broken packaging during transit
  • Order cancelled before dispatch
  • Prescription verification failure
  • Non-delivery despite delivery confirmation

Photographic evidence of damage should be submitted within 48 hours of delivery.

Refund Processing

Refund Method Processing Time
Hospital Wallet Credit (if applicable) Within 24 hours
UPI / Net Banking 2–3 business days
Debit / Credit Card 5–7 business days
EMI / Financing Services Subject to partner policies

07 Failed Deliveries

An order may be classified as a failed delivery when:

  • The delivery address is incorrect or incomplete.
  • The recipient is unavailable.
  • Access to the delivery location is restricted.
  • Delivery attempts are unsuccessful.

Our delivery partner may attempt redelivery once before returning the order to the pharmacy. Additional delivery charges may apply for reattempted deliveries.

08 Prescription Medicines

Prescription medicines require a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner.

Aabind Hospital follows the applicable requirements of the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940, the Pharmacy Act, 1948, and other applicable state and national healthcare regulations.

Prescription Requirements

  • Digital prescriptions may be uploaded through the patient portal or mobile application.
  • Original prescriptions may be required for certain regulated medicines.
  • Licensed pharmacists review prescriptions before dispensing.

Invalid Prescriptions

Forged, altered, expired, or invalid prescriptions may result in:

  • Immediate order cancellation
  • Refusal of service
  • Reporting to appropriate authorities where required by law

Repeat Medication Programs

Where available, Aabind Hospital may offer recurring medication or refill reminder services for chronic-care patients. Participation is voluntary and can be cancelled at any time.

09 Responsibility & Liability

Aabind Hospital's Role

Aabind Hospital facilitates pharmacy services and medicine delivery through authorized pharmacies and delivery partners.

Product Quality

The dispensing pharmacy is responsible for medicine authenticity, proper storage, product quality, and applicable regulatory compliance.

Patient Responsibility

Patients are responsible for providing accurate delivery information, maintaining valid prescriptions, and ensuring an authorized adult is available to receive medicines when required.

Force Majeure

Aabind Hospital shall not be liable for delays or delivery failures caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, government restrictions, public emergencies, strikes, and transportation disruptions.
