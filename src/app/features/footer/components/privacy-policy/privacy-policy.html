16. AI Services (OpenAI)

Aarogyam by Aabind provides AI-powered features for general health and wellness purposes. These features include:

An AI Assistant that answers general health and wellness questions.

An AI-powered food analysis feature that estimates approximate nutritional information, including calories and macronutrients, from food images.

When a user uses these AI features, the text entered into the AI Assistant and any food images selected for analysis are securely transmitted to OpenAI solely for the purpose of generating AI responses and nutritional estimates.

Users should not submit sensitive personal information, medical records, financial information, or information related to medical emergencies through these AI features.

The AI Assistant and Food Analysis features are intended to provide general health, wellness, and nutrition information only. They do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, emergency medical advice, or replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Nutritional information generated from food images is an estimate and may not be fully accurate.

OpenAI processes the submitted information only to generate AI responses and nutritional estimates in accordance with its applicable privacy and security practices. Aarogyam by Aabind shares only the information necessary to provide these AI-powered features.
